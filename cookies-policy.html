Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: May 1, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how Implify, Inc. ("Implify," "we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website www.implifyinc.com. By continuing to browse our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, improve user experience, and provide information to site owners.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (which expire when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them). They can also be "first-party cookies" (set by us) or "third-party cookies" (set by external services we use).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential operation — to enable core website functionality such as navigation and form submissions.
  • Performance and analytics — to understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it.
  • Preferences — to remember your settings and choices to provide a more personalized experience.
  • Marketing — to deliver relevant content and track the effectiveness of our outreach (only with your consent where required).

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary Required for the website to function. These cannot be disabled. Session
Analytics / Performance Collect anonymous data on page visits and traffic sources to help us improve site performance. Up to 2 years
Functional / Preferences Remember your preferences (e.g., language, region) for a better experience on return visits. Up to 1 year
Marketing / Targeting Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign effectiveness. Set only with consent. Up to 90 days

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are placed by trusted third-party services we use, including:

  • Google Analytics — for website usage analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag — for professional audience insights and ad performance tracking.
  • HubSpot / CRM tools — for tracking form interactions and lead management where applicable.

We do not control these third-party cookies. Please review the respective privacy policies of these services to understand how they use your data.

5. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have the right to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can manage cookies in the following ways:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies, or to notify you when a cookie is sent. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website. Below are links to cookie management instructions for common browsers:

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals websites not to track your browsing activity. Our website does not currently respond to DNT signals. We will update this policy if our practices change in this regard.
